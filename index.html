      About Us

      Protecting Human Lives at Workplace

      Founded in 1997, Vision Safety India is a professionally registered company delivering complete safety solutions across Marine, Industrial, Fire and Workplace Breathing Air, built on dedication, trust and integrity.

      We go far beyond supply. From consultancy and design to installation, testing, commissioning and NOC compliance, we are your single point of contact for complete fire and safety protection.

      Our Mission

      To be among India's best fire & safety suppliers, giving customers superior, quality safety equipment at cost-benefit options, with quality in every process from start to end.

      Our Vision

      To be India's most efficient fire & safety partner, so every customer has what they need to stay safe on the job, backed by the best sales and service of SCBA and PAPR.

          Our Services

          From the first drawing to the final certificate, and every service visit after.

          • Consultancy & Design

            Site surveys and fire protection system design matched to your risk and the applicable codes.

          • Supply & Installation

            Approved contractor for supply, installation and maintenance of fire protection systems.

          • Testing, Commissioning & NOC

            System testing, commissioning and support through fire NOC compliance.

          • Fire Extinguisher Servicing

            Inspection, refilling and servicing of all types of extinguishers at our service station.

          • SCBA & PAPR Service

            Sales, servicing and refilling of breathing apparatus and powered air purifying respirators.

          • Fire Detection & Alarm

            Testing and service of fire detection and alarm systems.

          • Hydrostatic Cylinder Testing

            Hydrostatic pressure testing of high pressure cylinders as per PESO.

          • Gas Monitor Calibration

            Calibration and certification of portable and fixed gas monitors.

          • Power Pack Servicing

            Servicing of power packs to keep your equipment ready when it matters.

          • Welding & Fabrication

            In-house welding work for fire system fittings and site requirements.

                CSR in Organic Farming

                Vision Farms

                We are fire & safety suppliers, but in spite of our busy schedule we also take care of the environment. It is time we learn to 'give back' and make our Earth green.

                At our farms in Solva-Raia and Neturlem, Sanguem, Goa, near the Neturlem bubbling lake, we have turned agricultural land into a productive resource with a lot of flora and fauna. And we work there just like we do at work: as Team Vision Safety.

                • 14acre farm, Solva-Raia & Neturlem
                • 7acre farm, Neturlem, Sanguem
                • 100%organic, with in-house natural manure

                Vegetables

                Radish, okra (lady fingers), local chillies, aubergine (brinjals), bitter gourd and long beans.

                Plantations

                A huge plantation of bananas, mangoes, coconuts, cashew, betelnut and kokum.

                Medicinal Plants

                Our thrill is experimenting with medicinal plants like Simarouba (Lakshmi Taru) and Soursop.

                Nature has long been known as a place to find tranquility and healing. At Vision Farms we are going back to this age-old agricultural tradition... naturally.
